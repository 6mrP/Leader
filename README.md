# Survey Results: Transformational Leadership and Emotional Regulation

This repository contains the data collection, processing, and analysis derived from surveys aimed at evaluating the relationship between **Transformational Leadership** and **Emotional Regulation** in organizational settings.

---

## 📌 Project Objectives

* **General:** To analyze how leaders' emotional regulation competencies influence the effectiveness and perception of transformational leadership.
* **Specific:**
  1. Identify key dimensions of emotional regulation applied to team management.
  2. Measure the level of adoption of transformational leadership practices within the selected sample.
  3. Establish statistical correlations between the variables using the collected data.

---

## 📊 Methodology and Instruments

Standardized instruments adapted to the study context were used for data collection:

* **Transformational Leadership Scale:** Assessment of dimensions such as idealized influence, inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, and individualized consideration.
* **Emotional Regulation Scale:** Measurement of strategies regarding cognitive reappraisal and the suppression of emotional expression.
